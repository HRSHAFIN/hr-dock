HR Dock {version} - install
=======================

1. Run "HR Dock Setup {version}.exe".

2. Windows will probably show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen.
   That is because this installer is not code-signed - signing needs a paid
   certificate, not because anything is wrong with it. Click
   "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Windows then asks whether to let the installer make changes. Click Yes.
   HR Dock installs into Program Files for everyone on this PC, which is
   why it needs that permission once. HR Dock itself runs as you, not as
   an administrator.

4. Choose a folder if you want a different one, and finish.

HR Dock then starts in the corner of your screen and lives in the system
tray. It has no taskbar button: use the tray icon, or Ctrl+Alt+A, to show
and hide it.


Upgrading from an older HR Dock
-------------------------------
Just run the new installer. If HR Dock is open, the installer says so and
closes it for you when you click OK. Any older copy is removed, whether it
was installed for you alone or for everyone.

Your data is kept: it lives in %APPDATA%\HR Dock, separate from the
program, and is upgraded in place on first launch.

If an older installer stopped with "HR Dock cannot be closed" or "Error
opening file for writing", this version fixes both. Click Cancel or Abort
on the old one, then run this one.


Uninstalling
------------
Settings > Apps > Installed apps > HR Dock > Uninstall. Your data folder is
left behind on purpose; delete %APPDATA%\HR Dock yourself if you want it gone.


What goes over the network
--------------------------
Your tasks, notes, routines and workouts never leave your machine. The app
makes three kinds of request, all for features you can see:

  - Weather, from Open-Meteo.
  - Your approximate location, from ipapi.co (falling back to ip-api.com),
    so the weather is for where you are. Search for a city in Settings and
    this stops.
  - The internet speed test, against Cloudflare - only when you press its
    button.
